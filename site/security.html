1. Core Security Invariants

ClockLend is built directly in native Solana SBF Rust (solana-program v2.2.1) without heavy frameworks or macro abstractions. The entire settlement pipeline adheres to formal cryptographic invariants:

  • Pure Integer Arithmetic (Float-Free): All interest calculations, LTV ratios, liquidation margins, and dividend distributions use 128-bit integer math with saturating additions and checked multiplications. Zero floating-point arithmetic guarantees 100% deterministic execution and immunity to IEEE-754 precision drift.
  • Fail-Closed AccountKind Discriminators: Every on-chain account incorporates an explicit 8-byte discriminator verified before any instruction unpacks data, completely preventing cross-type confusion attacks.
  • Isolated PDA Escrow Vaults: Collateral and principal never enter omnibus pools. Each loan and pawn offer derives unique Program Derived Addresses (PDAs) with cryptographically validated program authority seeds, preventing unauthorized fund drains.
  • Anti-Frontrunning Pre-Creation Defense: Escrow and vault initialization instructions assert program ownership and strict zero-lamport initialization checks, preventing griefing attacks where malicious actors pre-fund PDAs.
  • Fail-Closed Oracle Staleness Guard: Price feeds older than 600 seconds automatically halt borrow and liquidation operations, protecting users and liquidity providers from flash loan attacks and market dislocations. This guard is doing its job right now: at the time of writing the mainnet SOL and SKR feeds are stale, so borrows that read them revert until the keeper refreshes them (see the open ops finding in the round-14 report).
  • 24-Hour Social Grace Window: Borrowers past due receive a 24-hour protection window where social peers or community members can rescue the debt before liquidation triggers, stopping automated liquidation bot cascades. The window is not automatic: it starts only when the grace-period instruction is triggered on chain at or after the due time.

2. Protocol Revenue & Capital Distribution

ClockLend's monetization model is non-custodial on the lending side. Where revenue is distributed to token holders and reserves, note carefully who executes it — the split is enforced by the program, but the buyback is not:

  • Staker Dividend Stream & Continuous Linear Discount: When the SKR yield vault is initialized and appended to a borrow, the program routes a share of the origination fee (0.25% SOL / 0.50% USDC/SKR) into the SkrYieldVault at disbursement. If the vault token account is uninitialized or mismatched, the full fee routes to the treasury instead — the split is conditional, not guaranteed. Stakers claim proportional USDC dividends with a 1-hour anti-flash-loan harvest cooldown. In addition, staking 100 to 10,000+ SKR confers a continuous linear interest discount from 1.0% to 25.0% APR on borrowing. Stakers retain full non-custodial ownership of unbonded tokens with instant on-chain unstaking. As deployed, the mainnet yield vault is initialized but has never been funded.
  • Principal + 85% Interest & Desk Liquidity Withdrawals for LPs: Lending desk owners maintain isolated custody over their funds. Upon loan repayment, 100% of the loan principal and 85% of accrued interest return to the desk vault PDA. Desk creators retain full non-custodial ownership of idle capital and may withdraw unborrowed USDC back to their wallet at any time via the on-chain WithdrawLiquidity instruction.
  • 🔥 30% Protocol Revenue Buy & Burn: 30% of project revenue is committed to buying and burning $SKR (Jupiter DEX market buys plus direct SPL burns). This is a team-run buyback & burn program executed manually via scripts/burn-skr.mjs — it is not an autonomous on-chain mechanism, and it depends on a human operator running the script and on the treasury actually holding funds. No buyback has been executed to date.
  • Treasury: Remaining Protocol Fees — No Reserve or Insurance Mechanism: Everything not routed to the yield vault goes to the Treasury PDA: the 15% interest take-rate, the 5% liquidation margin, and origination fees whenever the yield-vault accounts are not supplied. There is no insurance or reserve mechanism in the program — nothing on chain holds or guarantees a reserve. As deployed, the treasury PDA has no account on chain, only a USDC token account at zero balance, and no protocol fee has ever been collected. What it holds is withdrawable by the team's admin key.

4. Mobile & Android Seeker Hardware Security

ClockLend was developed specifically for the Solana Seeker hardware ecosystem. Mobile security was graded D+ in round 14 and remediation is in progress — the client protections below are what the app implements:

  • Seed Vault Integration: Hardware-backed key generation and seed storage inside the device Secure Processing Unit (SPU). Transactions are signed in the enclave via Mobile Wallet Adapter rather than by a key held in JavaScript.
  • FLAG_SECURE Screen Shielding: Prevents unauthorized screen captures, screen recording trojans, and operating system task switcher leakage. (Note: on debug builds this flag is skipped.)
  • Anti-Tamper & Anti-Emulator Guard: The release build refuses execution on rooted hardware, Android emulators, or dynamic instrumentation tools (Frida, Xposed). Release-keystore guard coverage is narrower than originally intended — tracked as an open low finding.
  • RPC Dependency Disclosed: The client talks directly to Solana RPC/WebSocket endpoints and has no ClockLend backend server — it never routes user transactions through an app server. However, it does rely on third-party RPC infrastructure (Helius, plus public Solana RPC nodes). Those providers can see your IP address and the transactions you submit, and could in principle censor or delay them. This is a real, disclosed dependency, not "zero intermediaries".
